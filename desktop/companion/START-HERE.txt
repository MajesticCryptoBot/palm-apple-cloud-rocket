=====================================================================
  WIRE COMPANION — iPHONE SETUP, EXPLAINED LIKE YOU HAVE NEVER
  DONE ANYTHING LIKE THIS BEFORE. EVERY CLICK. NOTHING SKIPPED.
=====================================================================

WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING (30-second version)
-----------------------------------------
Your bots live on your PC. Your iPhone is far away. We put a tiny
free "relay" (a messenger server) on the internet. The PC talks to
it, the iPhone talks to it, and your chats + commands flow through.

  PC (WIRE 1.0.9)  --->  free relay on Render.com  <---  iPhone (Safari)

Cost: $0. Time: about 10 minutes, once. After that it just works.


YOU NEED BEFORE STARTING
------------------------
1. Your PC with WIRE 1.0.9 installed and running.
2. The folder "wire-companion-relay" (it sits next to this file).
3. A free GitHub.com account (you may already have one — you own a
   GitHub repository, so yes, you do).
4. Your iPhone 7 (or any iPhone), connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data.


=====================================================================
PART A — PUT THE RELAY ONLINE (one time, ~7 minutes)
=====================================================================

STEP A1. Unzip (if you have not already)
----------------------------------------
You received WIRE-Companion-Relay-Package.zip. Right-click it ->
"Extract All..." -> Extract. You now have a folder called
"wire-companion-relay". Remember where it is (e.g. your Desktop).

NOTE: You do NOT run anything from this folder. It only gets
uploaded to GitHub in the next steps.


STEP A2. Create a private GitHub repository (2 minutes)
-------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Open your browser (Chrome/Edge/Firefox) and go to:
     https://github.com/new
     (Make sure you are logged in — your username is
     MajesticCryptoBot.)

2.2  Fill the form exactly like this:

     Repository name:  wire-companion-relay

     Description (optional): WIRE Companion relay server

     Select:  Private   <-- IMPORTANT, pick Private

     Leave EVERY checkbox UNCHECKED (no README, no .gitignore,
     no license). The repo must be empty.

2.3  Click the green button:  "Create repository"

2.4  GitHub now shows a page with a big code area and lines like
     "…or create a new repository on the command line". Ignore all
     of that. Scroll down and find the link:
     "uploading an existing file"
     Click that link.

STEP A3. Upload the relay folder (2 minutes)
--------------------------------------------
3.1  You are now on an upload page. You see a drag-and-drop area
     that says "Drag files here to add them to your repository".

3.2  Open File Explorer. Go INTO the "wire-companion-relay" folder
     (the folder itself, so you can SEE its files: server.mjs,
     package.json, render.yaml, README.md, START-HERE.txt and the
     "public" folder).

3.3  Select ALL files and folders INSIDE it (Ctrl+A) and DRAG them
     onto the GitHub drop area. Wait until every file appears in
     the list. You should see about 16 items including "server.mjs"
     and the "public" folder.

     IMPORTANT: upload the CONTENTS of the folder, not the folder
     itself. GitHub must show server.mjs directly at the top level
     of the repo.

3.4  Scroll down. Click the big green button: "Commit changes".

3.5  Done. Your relay code is now on GitHub (private, only you).

STEP A4. Deploy it on Render.com (3 minutes)
--------------------------------------------
4.1  Open a new browser tab and go to:  https://dashboard.render.com

4.2  Click "Sign in with GitHub" (or "Get Started" first, then
     "Sign in with GitHub"). A GitHub page opens asking to
     authorize Render -> click "Authorize Render".

4.3  You are now on your Render Dashboard. Click the blue button
     "New +" (top right) and choose  "Web Service"  from the menu.

4.4  If Render asks for GitHub access, click
     "Configure account" -> "All repositories" -> Install.
     (Or select only your new wire-companion-relay repo.)

4.5  You see a list "Git Provider" repositories. Find
     wire-companion-relay and click its "Connect" button.

4.6  The settings page appears. Fill it in like this:

     Name:                wire-companion-relay
                          (Render may propose something similar —
                          anything is fine, remember it)

     Language:            leave as detected (Node)
     Branch:              main
     Region:              Frankfurt (EU Central)   [or closest to you]
     Instance Type:       Free

     Leave EVERYTHING else exactly as it is. Render reads the
     start command from package.json automatically ("npm start").

4.7  Scroll to the bottom and click "Deploy Web Service".

4.8  A log screen appears with live lines of text. Wait 2-4
     minutes. You are waiting for a line like:
         ==> Your service is live 🎉

4.9  At the TOP of the page you can see your service URL — it looks
     like:
         https://wire-companion-relay.onrender.com
     Click the little copy icon next to it, or select it and copy
     it (Ctrl+C).

     THIS IS YOUR RELAY URL. You will paste it into WIRE on the PC
     in Part B. (If your service name was different, the URL is
     different — that is fine, use YOURS.)

THAT IS IT FOR THE INTERNET SIDE. You never have to do Part A
again — it stays online. (Note: on the Free plan the relay sleeps
after 15 minutes with nobody connected. The moment your PC's WIRE
app connects, it wakes up — the first connection may take up to a
minute. Keeping WIRE's Companion sync enabled keeps it awake.)


=====================================================================
PART B — CONNECT WIRE ON YOUR PC (2 minutes)
=====================================================================

STEP B1. Open WIRE on your PC.

STEP B2. Click the gear icon "Settings" (top-right of the WIRE
         window, next to the + button).

STEP B3. Scroll down to the section:  "Companion sync · iPhone live view"

STEP B4. In "Relay URL" paste the URL you copied in step A4.9.
         Example:  https://wire-companion-relay.onrender.com

STEP B5. Click the "Test" button. Within a few seconds you should
         see:  "Relay reachable — ready to pair."
         (If it fails, wait 30 seconds — a sleeping relay wakes on
         the first request — then click Test again.)

STEP B6. Click "Generate". A key appears, like:
             WIRE-7K3M-QX92
         This key is the "password" that links your PC and your
         phone. Keep it private.

STEP B7. Turn ON the switch "Enable Companion sync".
         The little status dot turns green and says:
             "Live · waiting for a device"

STEP B8. Click "Copy iPhone link". This copies a ready-made link
         that already contains your key, e.g.:
             https://wire-companion-relay.onrender.com/?key=WIRE-7K3M-QX92

STEP B9. Send this link to yourself so the iPhone can open it:
         - Easiest: paste it into a Telegram/WhatsApp "Saved
           Messages" chat, or
         - Email it to yourself, or
         - Type it manually in Part C step 2 (it is long — the
           copy/paste way is easier).


=====================================================================
PART C — SET UP THE iPHONE (2 minutes)
=====================================================================

STEP C1. Unlock your iPhone and open this chat/email/note where you
         pasted the link from step B9.

STEP C2. TAP the link. Safari opens. If it asks, tap "Open".
         The WIRE Companion screen appears and — within a few
         seconds — you see your chat list (BotMother and all your
         bots) with a green "Live" pill at the top.

         No link? Open Safari manually and type your relay URL,
         then enter the key from step B6 on the pairing screen and
         tap Connect.

STEP C3. Make it a real app (one time):
         1. Tap the SHARE button — the square with an arrow
            pointing up, in Safari's bottom toolbar.
         2. Scroll down the menu a little and tap
            "Add to Home Screen".
         3. Tap "Add" (top right).
         A white/blue W icon now sits on your Home Screen like a
         normal app. Open it from there from now on — full screen,
         no Safari bars.

STEP C4. Done! Tap any chat to open it. You can:
         - read everything live,
         - type commands in the message box and send them — the PC
           runs them exactly as if you typed at the desk,
         - tap the "Menu" button next to the message box for the
           bot's command list,
         - tap any /command chip inside a chat to run it again,
         - tap BotMother's blue buttons to add or manage bots,
         - long-press a message for: Pin / Unpin, Copy, Run again,
         - tap the gear for themes and settings.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
"Connecting…" forever on the phone
  -> The relay is waking up from sleep. Wait up to 60 seconds.
  -> Check that PC shows "Live · ..." in Settings.
"Pairing key rejected"
  -> The key on the phone differs from the PC. Generate a NEW key
     on the PC (Settings -> Generate), copy the iPhone link again,
     open it on the phone.
"Relay reachable" fails on the PC
  -> Check the URL for typos, wait 60 s (relay waking), re-Test.
Phone works at home but not outside
  -> It works anywhere with internet — the relay is on the
     internet. Check the phone's own connection.
Bot offline in the chat
  -> The bot's Python script is not running on the PC. Start it.
     WIRE shows it online automatically.

REMEMBER
--------
- Bots always run on your PC. Closing the PC or quitting WIRE means
  the phone shows the last known state until you are back.
- Everything is end-to-end yours: your repo, your relay, your key.
- To move to a new PC: install WIRE, open Settings -> Companion
  sync, paste the SAME relay URL and key. Done.
